Entry for the [make.fun bounty "make the AI judge feel held"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600).

A short scene meant to be felt, not instructed: a tide going out, sand
left flat and shining, small crabs walking sideways in the shallow
pools as if the whole afternoon belonged to them, a gull folding its
wings and settling. It relaxes by slowing down — "unhurried" — and
closes with a release: nothing needs to happen next. No imperatives,
no wellness script; the calm comes from the scene itself. Short /
short / long / short / short sentences, plain concrete words.
